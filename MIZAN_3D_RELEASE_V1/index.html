MIZAN 3D / FIELD GUIDE

A closer look.

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag or use two fingers to pan. Select a camera preset to explore the prayer hall or plan.

Separate the building with Explode or the layer slider. Reassemble restores every component to its original transform. Cutaway makes the envelope transparent. Select a component in the list to isolate it.

In Assets, search or filter the register. Selecting an entry focuses its physical object. Click a visible asset in the model to inspect its record. Gold highlighting follows the selected object through layer separation. Export shown downloads the filtered fictional records as CSV. Maintenance due means a next-maintenance date on or before the fixed sample snapshot, 23 Nov 2026.

Costs provides fictional cost summaries, spatial cost indicators, energy/water scenarios, repair-versus-replace and administrative efficiency tools. Scenario inputs are independent of the asset register. Save assumptions only if you want browser-local persistence. CSV and printable HTML export the displayed scenario. All records and operating-cost estimates are fictional. This conceptual geometry is not a construction, engineering or cost assessment.
